Fund Recon

An expense labeler for Army Status of Funds reports. Budget analysts used to label every obligation line by hand each month. This tool remembers how each document number was labeled last time, applies it to the new report, and flags only what needs a person: new lines, amounts that changed, and charges from other units.

About

I graduated from West Point with a degree in economics and served as a logistics officer, with assignments in the 2nd Infantry Division in Korea and the 1st Special Forces Group. The job was keeping units supplied and budgets accounted for, usually with too little time and too many spreadsheets.

That is where I learned to look for the repeatable part of a task and automate it. Fund Recon started as a fix for a monthly reconciliation that took analysts days.

I build with AI as a working partner. I scope the problem, write the requirements and test cases, and use Claude to help write and review code, run data pipelines and check results against the source. I care about the unglamorous parts: access control, data that stays correct, and tools a non-technical teammate can use on day one.
